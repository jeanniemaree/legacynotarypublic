# Client Off-Page SEO Checklist
To solidify your A++ SEO score and establish total dominance in the 77566 region, the final step requires claiming your business on the top local directories. Your code is now fully prepared to map directly to these entities.

Please complete these three steps:

### 1. Google Business Profile (GBP)
1. Go to [Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/).
2. Claim or create the profile for **"Legacy Notary Public"**.
3. Ensure the address is listed exactly in **Lake Jackson, TX 77566**.
4. Link the website directly to `https://legacynotarypublic.com/`.

### 2. Apple Business Connect
1. Go to [Apple Business Connect](https://businessconnect.apple.com/).
2. Claim your business to ensure you appear correctly on Apple Maps and Siri searches.
3. Use the exact same name, address, and phone number as your Google profile.

### 3. Bing Places for Business
1. Go to [Bing Places](https://www.bingplaces.com/).
2. Click **"Sync with Google My Business"** (this is a 1-click sync once your Google profile is approved).
3. This sync ensures Bing, Yahoo, and Microsoft Copilot AI have your exact data.

### 4. Update the Website Code
Once you have the public URLs for your Google, Apple, and Bing profiles, open `src/config/siteConfig.ts` and paste the URLs into the `sameAs` array. This creates a "closed loop" of trust between your website and your off-page profiles, which Google's algorithm rewards heavily.
